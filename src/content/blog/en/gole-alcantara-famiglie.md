The Alcantara Gorges have a reputation as an adventurous destination, with walks through the riverbed and cold water year-round — but you don't need to tackle the more demanding canyon to enjoy them as a family. With a little care, it's one of the easiest trips to organise from Pedara even with young children, thanks to routes designed specifically for those who don't want or can't take on the more adventurous stretches.

## The easiest routes

The Alcantara River Park, near Motta Camastra, offers a system of equipped walkways and paths that let you admire the canyon's basalt walls up close without having to go down into the riverbed or get wet. These are flat routes, suitable even for strollers on the main stretches, that still offer an up-close view of the volcanic rock's distinctive columnar formation.

For families with slightly older children who still want to get close to the water without tackling the more demanding stretches of the canyon, the lifts and stairs leading down from car park level to the river allow safe access to the banks, where you can dip your feet or simply watch the water up close, without having to wade upstream like more experienced hikers.

## What to consider with young children

Safety remains the priority: rocks near the river can be slippery, and children should always be watched closely in the stretches closest to the water, where the current, although generally moderate, can catch you by surprise. The equipped walkways remain the calmest option for anyone travelling with young children or those less experienced moving over uneven natural terrain.

To avoid the crowds and make the visit more manageable with children, it's worth arriving early in the morning: besides avoiding queues at the ticket office, you also avoid the midday heat, particularly hard on young children in summer. Planning frequent stops, without the pressure of trying to see the whole park in a short time, helps keep the outing enjoyable even for those who tire more easily — a short, relaxed visit beats a long, rushed one.

## Nearby

Near the main park entrance there are usually rest areas and small kiosks where you can take a break with the children, handy for a snack or a cool drink without having to interrupt the visit to go back to the car. Some areas of the park also offer quieter spaces, away from the main flow of visitors, where you can settle down for a picnic in the shade.

A trip to the Alcantara Gorges with children only requires a bit more care than a visit among adults alone, but it remains one of the easiest experiences to organise starting from Magma 610: book your stay in Pedara and bring the whole family to discover this unique corner of Sicily.
