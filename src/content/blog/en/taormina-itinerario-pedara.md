Taormina can be explored at a relaxed pace even in a single day, as long as you plan your timing well. Starting from Pedara, home to Magma 610, you can comfortably see the main stops — Greek Theatre, old town and Isola Bella — and be back by evening without rushing. Here's an hour-by-hour itinerary designed exactly for this kind of trip.

## Morning: Greek Theatre and old town

Setting off from Pedara around 8:00-8:30 am, you'll reach Taormina in about 30-35 minutes, in time to get to the Greek Theatre shortly after opening. Arriving between 9:00 and 9:30 am lets you visit it in relative peace, before the organised tour groups and coach crowds arrive in numbers, usually from mid-morning onward. The theatre deserves at least 45 minutes to an hour, between exploring the cavea and taking in the view of Etna and the sea that opens up from the ancient stage — one of Sicily's most iconic sights.

Leaving the theatre, around 10:30-11:00 am you can start exploring the old town along Corso Umberto, the pedestrian street running through the city from Porta Messina to Porta Catania. A couple of hours are enough to stroll at ease among the baroque churches, medieval palaces and panoramic terraces that appear unexpectedly along the way, with a recommended stop at Piazza IX Aprile, one of the city's most photographed viewpoints.

## Lunch and a stroll

Around 1:00 pm, after visiting the old town, it's the natural moment for a lunch break: the side streets off Corso Umberto offer several options, from simple eateries to terraces with panoramic views over the gulf. Taking about an hour and a half for lunch lets you rest your legs before the second part of the day, dedicated to Isola Bella.

## Afternoon: Isola Bella

Around 3:00 pm, once lunch is done, head toward Isola Bella, reachable by scenic cable car from the centre of Taormina or with a longer walk down the switchbacks toward the sea — the cable car remains the most practical option for anyone who's already walked a lot during the morning. Isola Bella is a small island connected to the coast by a sandbar, with turquoise water and Mediterranean vegetation: a couple of hours, between the beach and a walk around the island, are enough to enjoy it at leisure, even just for a quick swim in the warmer months.

Around 5:30-6:00 pm, riding the cable car back up toward the centre, you can treat yourself to one last stop for a coffee or a granita before heading back toward Pedara. The return to Magma 610, allowing for late-afternoon traffic, usually takes 35-40 minutes — enough to be back in time for dinner, with an intense but well-organised day behind you.
