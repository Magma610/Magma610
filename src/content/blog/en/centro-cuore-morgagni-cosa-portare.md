Packing well before an extended stay near the Centro Cuore Morgagni makes everything easier once you arrive — especially when your days are already shaped by medical appointments and you want to avoid the hassle of having to track down something you forgot. Here's a practical checklist designed specifically for anyone planning a longer-than-usual stay at Magma 610.

## Documents and practical necessities

A valid ID is naturally essential, as is your health insurance card and any medical documentation related to the reason for your stay — previous reports, prescriptions, any documentation required for appointments already scheduled. It's worth keeping all this material in an easily accessible folder, separate from the rest of your luggage, so it's always at hand without having to dig through suitcases before every visit.

If the stay involves caring for a hospitalised family member, it can also help to bring their essential personal items — not just for the hospital stay, but also for any moments when it's possible to take them temporarily outside the ward, if conditions allow.

## What you need for the kitchen/laundry

Magma 610's apartments already come equipped with a fully-fitted kitchen and a washing machine, so there's no need to bring appliances or basic utensils. It is, however, worth bringing any specific products you're used to — a particular type of coffee, particular spices, personal care products that aren't always easy to find in local shops — especially if your stay runs longer than a few days and you'd rather not have to source everything locally during the first, already demanding, days.

For anyone expecting a stay of several weeks, having a small supply of laundry detergent or personal care products already on hand can be convenient, avoiding an extra expense in the first days of your stay, when attention is naturally focused elsewhere.
