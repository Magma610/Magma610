Not every guest looking for accommodation in Pedara is here on holiday. Anyone with a scheduled medical visit, a hospital admission, or a family member to accompany during a stay at the Centro Cuore Morgagni has different needs: no activity package or sightseeing tips required — what's needed is a practical, quiet, nearby place to settle into without extra worries in the days before or after a medical appointment.

Magma 610 was built with this in mind too. Our two independent apartments, Magma 102 and Magma 104, are just minutes from the Centro Cuore Morgagni, and over the years have often been chosen by guests organising a stay tied to a cardiology visit or an admission, for themselves or a family member.

## How far it is

The Centro Cuore Morgagni is about 5 minutes by car from Magma 610, or a roughly 15-minute walk. It's a distance that makes a real practical difference: there's no need to drive for every trip, and anyone returning several times in the same day — or accompanying a family member repeatedly during an admission — can do so without dealing with traffic or parking in an unfamiliar area each time.

For those arriving from out of town, often already carrying a lot on their mind, knowing they can reach the medical centre on foot, or that it's just a very short drive away, removes a worry that in other circumstances would weigh more than expected.

## Why an independent apartment helps at times like this

An independent apartment, compared to a more traditional hospitality option, offers an advantage that matters more than usual at times like this: no fixed schedules. Anyone who needs to get up very early for a visit, or come back late after hours at the hospital, doesn't have to work around breakfast times or rules designed for a holiday stay.

The equipped kitchen makes it possible to prepare a light meal before an appointment, or simply avoid eating out every day during a family member's extended hospital stay — a small detail, but one that, for someone already in a stressful situation, can make the difference between a manageable stay and one that's more taxing than it needs to be.

The quiet of Pedara, a town without the traffic and bustle of a bigger city, is also something many guests in this situation particularly appreciate compared to staying in central Catania.

We understand that the needs of anyone staying near the Centro Cuore Morgagni for a visit or an admission rarely match standard check-in and check-out times. When circumstances call for it, we try to accommodate different arrival or departure times, and it's possible to arrange short stays, even a single night, or extended ones, based on the timing of medical visits.

If you're dealing with a particular situation — an imminent admission, visiting hours not yet confirmed, or simply need to talk to someone before booking — please get in touch directly.
