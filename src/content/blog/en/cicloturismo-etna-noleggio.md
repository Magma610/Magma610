You don't need to travel with your own bicycle to enjoy cycle touring on Etna. Anyone arriving at Magma 610 by plane, or who simply prefers not to deal with transporting their own bike, can rely on several rental points in the area, designed exactly for those who want to ride the volcano without organising everything from home.

## Where to rent

In the area around Etna's south side there are several rental points, offering both traditional bikes — road or gravel, suited to those with experience — and e-bikes, increasingly popular among those wanting to tackle the volcano's climbs without necessarily being in top form. Many of these services also offer add-on accessories, such as helmets, water bottles or small repair kits, handy for anyone who'd rather not bring everything from home.

In high season — especially spring and early summer, when the weather is most favourable for cycle touring — the availability of rental bikes can drop quickly, especially on weekends. It's worth checking availability and booking a few days ahead, particularly for e-bikes, generally the most requested for their versatility on climbing routes.

## What to consider when choosing

The choice between a traditional bike and an e-bike depends mainly on the type of route you want to tackle. For the more demanding climbs toward Etna's mid-to-high altitudes, where elevation gain can comfortably exceed a thousand metres, an e-bike is generally the more practical choice even for trained cyclists, letting you enjoy the scenery without the extreme effort of the steepest gradients. For the flat routes among the hillside towns on the south side, on the other hand, a traditional bike remains perfectly adequate.

It's worth checking the available sizes at the time of booking, especially for those outside the average height range: not all rental points carry a wide range of sizes, and booking ahead lets you secure the right fit instead of having to make do with whatever's left at the last minute.

## Where to start from

Pedara, home to Magma 610, is a convenient base for anyone renting a bike in the area: Etna's south side is directly accessible from the town, with no need to transport the rented bike over long distances before starting your route. Many rental points in the area also offer delivery and pick-up directly at your accommodation, a convenient service for anyone wanting to keep logistics to a minimum and focus purely on riding.
