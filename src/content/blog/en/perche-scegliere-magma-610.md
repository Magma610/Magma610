Anyone looking for accommodation in Pedara has plenty of options. Here's why Magma 610 could be the right one.

## Steps from the centre, not out in the countryside

Many holiday homes in the area are tucked away in the Etna countryside — beautiful, but isolated. Magma 610 is different: it's just 300 metres from Pedara's main square, an easy walk away. Bars, restaurants, shops and the life of the town are right there, with no need for a car for every small errand.

## Direct management, not a hotel

There's no reception desk, no call centre. Whoever manages Magma 610 answers requests in person, knows the area well, and can give genuine advice on where to go — from Etna's trails to the wineries, from the Alcantara Gorges to Taormina.

## Two independent solutions

Magma 102 and Magma 104 are two self-contained apartments, each with its own entrance, amenities and character — not hotel rooms, but private spaces where you can feel at home. Both share a characteristic lava-stone outdoor courtyard, the quiet heart of the accommodation.

## The ideal base for exploring Etna

Etna's south side, where Pedara is located, is one of the most convenient areas for exploring the volcano: craters, trails and historic wineries are just minutes away by car. There's no need to move around every day — Magma 610 stays a convenient fixed point for your whole stay.

## Also for those not here for tourism

Magma 610 is just 5 minutes by car (15 on foot) from the Centro Cuore Morgagni — a detail that matters to anyone looking for comfortable, quiet accommodation for a medical visit or to stay close to a family member, not just to hikers.
