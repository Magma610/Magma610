When you book accommodation through a large platform, there are often several layers between the guest and the host: messages routed through a centralised system, standard policies designed for millions of different properties, an experience that tends to feel the same everywhere. With Magma 610 things work a bit differently: behind every enquiry is Veronique, who replies in person, knows both apartments in detail, and knows the surrounding area like few others.

It's not just a matter of convenience: booking directly, or simply knowing you have a direct line to whoever manages the accommodation, changes the way you experience your stay from the very first exchange of messages.

## How it works today

At the moment, bookings for Magma 102 and Magma 104 go through Airbnb, with a direct link for each of the two apartments. It remains the simplest channel for anyone wanting to book today: a calendar updated in real time, secure payment through the platform, and the ability to message Veronique directly with any question before confirming.

Even going through Airbnb, management stays entirely direct: there's no intermediary agency answering on behalf of the property, and enquiries always reach the same person, who will later welcome guests in person or arrange check-in.

## What direct management means

Direct management means, above all, speed and personalisation. Questions about arrival times, special requests or doubts about the area get quick answers, without going through a generic customer service team that may not even know Pedara. Anyone asking for advice on where to eat, which Etna trail to choose, or how to plan a day at the Alcantara Gorges gets suggestions based on real, local knowledge, not standard replies.

Flexibility, too — check-in and check-out times, special requests tied to a short or extended stay, specific needs like those of guests staying nearby for a visit to the Centro Cuore Morgagni — can be discussed directly, without having to follow rigid rules designed for the average guest.
