Magma 610 is made up of two independent apartments, Magma 102 and Magma 104, both set within the same lava-stone building in the heart of Pedara. While they share the same location and the same spirit, they have different features — designed for slightly different types of stay. Here's a practical guide to help you understand which one suits you best.

## Magma 104

Magma 104 is designed mainly for couples or those travelling in pairs: it accommodates up to 2 guests, with a separate bedroom featuring 1 bed and a private bathroom. It's the most suitable option for anyone looking for a bit more privacy during their stay, with the sleeping area kept separate from the apartment's other spaces.

The carefully finished interiors, the equipped outdoor courtyard shared with the other apartment, and the location just steps from Pedara's centre remain the same as Magma 102 — what changes substantially is the layout of the space, designed here for a more intimate stay.

## Magma 102

Magma 102, on the other hand, is a studio, designed for a small group or a slightly larger family: it accommodates up to 3 guests, with the sleeping area and living area sharing one bright, single space, for a total of 2 beds and a private bathroom. It's the better choice for anyone who doesn't need a separate bedroom and prefers a single, versatile space, perhaps for a more informal stay.

One practical detail worth noting: unlike Magma 104, Magma 102 doesn't have a travel cot — something to consider for anyone travelling with an infant who'd prefer to find one already included.

## What they have in common

Beyond the differences, the two apartments share far more than what sets them apart. Both look onto the same characteristic lava-stone outdoor courtyard, the quiet heart of the accommodation, where guests of both apartments can enjoy time outdoors away from the traffic of the centre. Both are in the same location, just 300 metres from Pedara's main square, convenient both for those wanting to walk to bars and restaurants and as a starting point for exploring Etna and the surrounding area.

The basic amenities — equipped kitchen, linens included, Wi-Fi connection — are also equivalent between the two apartments, as is the direct management by Veronique, always personally reachable for either accommodation.

Whether the choice falls on Magma 104 for a more intimate couple's stay, or Magma 102 for a small group preferring a single space, check the availability of both and choose the one best suited to your needs.
